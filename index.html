Trademark Rights & Designations

Use of PFL official marks across all marketing platforms. Official UK Betting Partner for PFL Lyon (or mutually agreed designation); non-exclusive "Official Partner of PFL".

◉

Virtual Overlay Branding

Virtual logo placement on one large canvas, one vertical bumper and one inner middle canvas position. Limited to feeds distributed solely within the UK.

▤

Social Content Distribution

Two social media posts with Lottoland logo inclusion and tag on each. First-time depositor betting offer. Lottoland featured on the Sponsor and How to Bet sections of PFLmma.com.

▶

Digital & Video Content

Pre-, during- and post-event assets: press conferences, weigh-ins, face-offs, promos and media-day cutdowns; rapid-turn highlights; official photos and recap videos for Lottoland-owned channels.

↗

Watch & Bet / Data

Non-exclusive live-event Watch & Bet rights for the event, plus the official PFL data feed via Sportradar, to the extent available.

★

Athlete Ambassador Program Additional Cost

One PFL Lyon fighter as event ambassador — digital content support, fight-kit logo inclusion, appearances and autograph signings, and other mutually agreed roles. Priced separately — not included in the £40,000 commitment.

◆

VIP Hospitality & Access

Three VIP tickets and five GA tickets. On-stage and meet-and-greet at weigh-ins, hosted backstage tour on fight night, and invitations to press conferences and face-offs.

☰

Account Management

A dedicated PFL Account Director and point-of-contact for turnkey execution of the partnership each year.
